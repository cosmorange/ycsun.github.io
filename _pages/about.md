Hello! I am currently a **first-year Ph.D. student** in the [Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE)](https://www.ece.rutgers.edu/) at [Rutgers University–New Brunswick](https://www.rutgers.edu/), advised by [Prof. Yingying Chen](https://www.winlab.rutgers.edu/~yychen/).  

Previously, I obtained my **bachelor's degree** from [Wuhan University](https://www.whu.edu.cn/), where I worked as a research assistant in [Prof. Qian Wang](https://nisplab.whu.edu.cn/People.htm)’s [NIS&P Lab](https://nisplab.whu.edu.cn/).  

My research interests include **security and privacy for mobile systems**, **LLMs**, and **robust and trustworthy ML/AI systems**.
